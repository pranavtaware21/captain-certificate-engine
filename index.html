Captain Certificate Engine

Upload a CSV, get every captain's Certificate of Excellence back as one ZIP.

CSV format

Three columns, one row per captain. First row must be the header.

nametagachievement
Ravi Kumar Punctuality Pro 100% on-time across 62 trips this month
Sunil Yadav Elite Captain perfect 100 out of 100 with zero commuter complaints
  • name — required. Prints as the captain's name. Rows with no name are skipped.
  • tag — optional, 1–2 words. Prints as the gold pill. Leave blank for no pill.
  • achievement — optional, one short sentence, lower case, no full stop. Leave blank to drop the “Awarded for” block.

Download template.csv · filled-in example (5 rows)

Other header names work too (captain_name, badge, citation, reason…) — and you can remap any column after upload.
