The CSV

Three columns, one row per captain. First row is the header.

nametagachievement
Ravi Kumar Punctuality Pro 100% on-time across 62 trips this month
Sunil Yadav Elite Captain perfect 100 out of 100, clean on every pillar
nameRequired. Prints as the captain's name. Rows without one are skipped and reported.
tagOptional. One or two words — set large in gold italic under “Awarded for”. Blank moves the achievement up into that line.
achievementOptional. One short sentence, printed on the line under the tag. Blank leaves just the tag.

Download template.csv Filled-in example Other headers work too — captain_name, badge, citation, reason
